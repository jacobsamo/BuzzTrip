We're excited to introduce BuzzTrip 1.2, featuring our most requested feature: **Paths**. You can now draw directly on your maps — trace routes, outline areas, measure distances, and bring your maps to life with custom shapes and lines.

## Draw Directly on Your Maps

Whether you're planning a mapping a hiking trail, or outlining regions of interest, Paths gives you the creative freedom to visualize your journey exactly how you imagine it.

### What You Can Do

- **Draw Lines** - Trace routes or any path you want to highlight
- **Create Shapes** - Outline areas, regions, or zones with custom polygons
- **Add Circles** - Mark radius areas around specific locations
- **Customize Everything** - Change colors and styles to match your map's theme
- **Edit Anytime** - Click any path to modify, reshape, or update it

### Perfect For

- **Outdoor Adventures** - Trace hiking trails, bike paths, or running routes
- **Area Mapping** - Outline neighborhoods, districts, or regions of interest

The best part? Paths sync in real-time with your collaborators, so everyone working on your map sees updates instantly.

## Better First Experience

We've improved the onboarding experience to get you started faster:

- **Starter Map Created** - When you sign up, we automatically create your first map with helpful collections to organize your places
- **Guided Experience** - Default collections help you organize your map from day one
